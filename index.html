Partnership Opportunities

Four ways to invest with us.

Structured entry points into Africa's growing healthcare sector — from co-owning private hospitals to upgrading public infrastructure.

01

Co-Ownership of Private Hospitals

Co-own and co-develop new hospitals and specialized centers. We bring clinical expertise and operations — you bring the capital.

  • Joint equity ownership in new developments
  • Oncology, dialysis, surgical & aesthetic centers
  • Multi-country portfolio: Kenya, Ghana, Morocco+
  • Full operational management by our team
02

Operations & Management Contracts

We assume full operational control of existing hospitals, transforming underperforming facilities while owners retain the property.

  • Clinical, financial, HR & procurement management
  • Performance-based profit sharing
  • International quality standards
  • Transparent financial reporting
03

Public-Private Partnerships

We structure PPP agreements with governments to modernize public hospitals — proven across 15 African countries.

  • Revenue-sharing with government authorities
  • Public-sector compliant O&M frameworks
  • Long-term concession models
  • Capacity building & knowledge transfer
04

Equipment & Technology Leasing

Access world-class diagnostics and surgical technology through structured leasing, without prohibitive upfront costs.

  • CT, MRI, dialysis & surgical systems
  • Revenue-share tied to utilization
  • Turnkey procurement to integration
  • Multi-hospital network pricing
