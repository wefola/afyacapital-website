Who We Are

We don't just invest.
We operate.

Afya Capital LLC is a US-based holding company building Africa's healthcare infrastructure — pairing patient capital with hands-on operational mastery across five nations.

Our Story

A proven operator across African healthcare.

Afya Capital was founded on a simple conviction: Africa's healthcare future will be built by those who can both fund facilities and run them to international standards. From a US base in Delaware, we have grown a portfolio of hospitals, oncology centers, dialysis units, and specialized medical facilities across sub-Saharan and North Africa.

With hands-on experience managing 40+ hospitals, we deliver clinical excellence alongside strong, transparent financial returns — and we structure every partnership for accountability and long-term value.

Operators, not just allocators

We appoint leadership, run clinical operations, and own the outcome — capital backed by accountability.

Impact and returns, aligned

Every facility we build heals communities while delivering industry-leading profitability to our partners.

Institutional discipline

Transparent reporting, international quality frameworks, and governance investors can trust.

Our Mission

Healthier nations, built to last.

Across Africa, demand for quality care is outpacing the infrastructure to deliver it. We exist to close that gap — developing and operating facilities that raise the standard of care while generating durable returns for the partners who make them possible.

Our model is asset-light by design: partners own the infrastructure, we run it, and value is shared through performance.
